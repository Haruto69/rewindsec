Prototype tooling

Session

Event arrival

Releases the next authored arrival immediately. The server does the delivering, on the same path the timeline uses, so nothing arrives that could not have arrived on its own.

Advance simulation time

Moves the server clock forward and fires whatever becomes due, in the order and at the simulation times it was already scheduled for. It compresses waiting, never causality. There is no control here that invents a consequence: to see a chain, take the decision that causes it.

Integrity controls

Copy, cut and paste are blocked everywhere on a learner surface except Notes. PrintScreen raises the notice on its own where the browser reports the key; this button shows the same screen without needing the key to arrive.
